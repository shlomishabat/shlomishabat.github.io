About

I'm a cybersecurity researcher and software engineer specializing in web privacy. My day-to-day is reverse-engineering how sites and third parties track users — fingerprinting scripts, tracking pixels, session recorders — and building the crawlers, browser instrumentation, and data pipelines that surface that evidence at internet scale. I like tools that are small, sharp, and honest about what they do.
