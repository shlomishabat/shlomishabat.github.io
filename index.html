About

I'm a cyber security researcher based in Tel Aviv, currently at Darrow AI and previously at PerimeterX (now Human Security) and K2view. The thread running through my career is distinguishing humans from machines: I spent years in bot detection, separating real users from automation, and my research now applies that same lens to the newest non-human actor, the AI agent. My work sits where research meets engineering: reverse engineering complex web applications, building detection logic, and turning one-off findings into production pipelines.
